## Art production and validation rule correction

- `AGENTS.md` and `docs/HARNESS.md` were updated after user correction on 2026-06-13.
- New rule: when an existing `artSample/` image is the target, treat it as a reproduction target, not a creative prompt or loose mood reference.
- Required workflow for art/modeling/texturing:
  - Break the target image into silhouette, proportions, major forms, individual parts, surface material, wear pattern, lighting, and camera angle.
  - In Blender or the relevant DCC tool, match the reference camera render first before claiming the model is ready.
  - Use side-by-side render comparison as the quality gate.
  - Renderer counts, object existence, FBX/GLB export existence, material assignment, and internal validation logs are supporting checks only.
  - If a 2D reference does not show backsides, interiors, exact dimensions, or mechanical details, derive them from `docs/GAME_DESIGN_SOURCE.txt` or explicitly document the inference instead of inventing visible design changes.
  - Texture work must include visible material qualities such as albedo variation, chipped paint, dirt, roughness/metalness response, and normal/bump detail when the reference shows worn rough surfaces.
